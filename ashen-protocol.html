A VR survival-horror shooter · Chapters 1–2 of 6

ASHEN PROTOCOL

Chapter 1 — Dead Streets  ·  Chapter 2 — The Underground

You are Cinder, a hunter dropped into a ruined city to find out what ended it. A single voice on the radio, Wren, is the only thing keeping you company. Draw from your holster, reload from your belt, rack the slide yourself — and look closely at who built this nightmare.

Draw pistol — reach to your holster (hip) and squeeze grip
Fire — trigger on the gun hand
Reload — A/X ejects the mag · grab a fresh one from your belt pouch with the other hand · push it into the grip · B/Y drops the slide (or grab and pull the slide back)
Knife — grip at the sheath on your off-hand side, then slash or stab
Move — left stick · Turn — right stick
Workbench — touch a green pad with your hand and squeeze the trigger to spend points (kills and recovered notes earn them)
Headlamp — A/X on the off hand when not reloading
Settings

Contains graphic violence, gore and horror themes. Play seated or standing with room to move; take breaks. Best on Meta Quest in the Quest Browser (WebXR). Audio is generated live — turn your volume up a little.
