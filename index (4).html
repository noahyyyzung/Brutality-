QUEST 3 CONTROLS
Grip (hold)Fly fist-first where that hand points. Both grips = boost. Near a person, car or enemy = grab
Release gripThrow what you're holding (swing your arm!)
TriggerUse that hand's power. Lasers: tap = bolt, hold = continuous beam (once unlocked)
A / XSwitch that hand's power
B / YOpen the Skill Web, Suit Builder, Stats and Settings (game pauses)
Left stickWalk / drift while hovering
Right stickSmooth turn
Swing fistsPunch enemies, cars and buildings. Fly fast into buildings to smash through
