QUEST 3 CONTROLS
Grip (hold)Fly fist-first where that hand points. Both grips = boost. Near a person, car or enemy = grab
Release gripThrow what you're holding (swing your arm!)
TriggerUse that hand's power. Lasers: tap = bolt, hold = continuous beam (once unlocked)
A / XTap = switch that hand's power. HOLD = Pick your hero! (Undefeated or Web Weaver)
MAD STEEL: Grip = palm thruster (point hands DOWN to rise, BACK to fly forward) · Trigger = repulsor · Missiles: HOLD trigger and LOOK at enemies to lock on, release to fire · Airstrike: point and pull trigger
WEB WEAVER: Grip = shoot a swing web (let go to release) · Yank that hand back = ZIP forward · Web an enemy = fly at them for a big hit · Trigger = web shot · Right stick up / stick click = jump · Walk into walls to crawl (once unlocked)
B / YOpen the Skill Web, Suit Builder, Stats and Settings (game pauses)
Left stickWalk / drift while hovering
Right stickSmooth turn
Swing fistsPunch enemies, cars and buildings. Fly fast into buildings to smash through
DESKTOP — WASD move · mouse look · Space fly/rise · Shift boost · C descend · LMB/RMB powers · F punch · G grab/throw · Q/E switch powers · Tab menu
Click to look around · Tab = menu · Esc = free mouse
